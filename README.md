# AutoGas Store Manager

The Autogas Store Manager is an inventory Management service developed for Automation Autogas Company as a subsidiary arm of the Automation and Engineering Academy.

The application was developed to help AAC:
- Keep track of Engineering materials and equipment
- Manage access to the company store through authentication and authorisation practices powered by this application
- Perform accountability measures to ensure stock reports reflect actual inventory
- Plan stocking actions, inventory audits and equipment maintenance or expiry

Soem additional features:
- Scheduling of CNG conversions by Customers
- Estimates for CNG conversion
- Customer-Technician communication concerning formal issues or conversion schedule negotiation
- Availability of maerials and personel for conversion

PS: Features common with inventory applications, especially those for engineering firms, can also be implemented
